About Convx

What is Convx?

A free music app for Android and Windows. It plays from YouTube Music, shows word-synced lyrics, blends songs together with AutoMix, and wraps it all in liquid glass. Aryan (Cosmic Taser) makes it; the Windows version is maintained by m33md.

Is it really free?

Yes. Convx is free and open source under the GPL-3.0, for anyone to use, read and build on. If you’d like to support it, there’s Aryan’s Ko-fi.

Is Convx made by YouTube or Google?

No. Convx is an independent project and isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube or Google.

Is there a version for iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Convx is on Android and Windows today. News about anything new lands first on the Discord.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Convx works without signing in to anything. Sign in to YouTube Music if you want your own library, playlists and recommendations.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is Convx safe?

That message appears for apps that aren’t from a big publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway. Convx is built in the open on GitHub, so anyone can check the code and how each download is made.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or newer for the PC app, and Android 8.0 or newer for the phone app.

How do I update?

On Windows, Convx checks for a new version each time it opens and updates itself from inside the app. You can also do it in Settings › Help & About › App updates.

Music

Where does the music come from?

From YouTube Music. Modules can add other sources, often in lossless FLAC, and Convx asks them first, with YouTube Music as the fallback.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Download a song from its menu, or turn on Auto download on like. Downloads are in Library under Downloaded and play with no connection.

Can I bring my Spotify playlists?

Yes, two ways. Link Spotify in Settings › Accounts & Services › Spotify, or, without Premium, export your playlists as CSV files from exportify.net and import them in Settings › Privacy & Storage › Backup & restore. Apple Music playlists can be imported from a link too.

A song won’t play. What can I do?

Convx asks YouTube for a fresh link and tries again on its own. If a song still won’t play, YouTube may be holding it back for a while, so try again later, or add a module that can play it from somewhere else.

Lyrics

Why aren’t some lyrics word-synced?

Convx asks every lyrics source at once and shows the first good answer, then switches to a word-timed version if one arrives. Some songs only have line-by-line lyrics anywhere. You can change which sources Convx asks in Settings › Player & Lyrics › Sources & priority.

Can lyrics be translated?

Yes. Add a key from an AI provider in Settings › Player & Lyrics › AI lyrics translation, then turn on translation from the lyrics menu in the player. Each song keeps its translation, so it’s only paid for once per language. Romanized lyrics need no key.

Listening together

Can my phone and my PC listen together?

Yes. Start a party on either one and share the code. Phones and PCs join with the same codes and hear the same song at the same moment.

How do I show what I’m playing on Discord?

Keep the Discord app open on your PC and turn on Discord in Settings › Accounts & Services › Discord. Your status shows the song while it plays and goes away when you pause.

Your data

Does Convx collect my data?

No. There is no Convx account and no Convx server keeping your library. The Privacy page lists every service Convx talks to and when.

How do I move my library to a new PC?

Make a backup in Settings › Privacy & Storage › Backup & restore and restore it on the new PC. It carries your library, playlists, history and settings. Sign in again on the new PC, since sign-ins never leave the old one.

Help

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

On the Convx Discord, or as a GitHub issue: Android or Windows. On Windows, Settings › Help & About › Crash report shows what went wrong the last time Convx closed unexpectedly, which helps a lot.

Still stuck? The Docs walk through every part of Convx for Windows, and the Discord is full of people happy to help.
