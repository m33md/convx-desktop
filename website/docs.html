Install

On Windows

  1. Download Convx-Setup-<version>.exe from the latest release.
  2. Run it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Convx from the Start menu.

The same page has an .msi installer, and a portable .zip that runs without installing. Convx needs Windows 10 or newer.

On Android

  1. Download the APK from Convx’s releases.
  2. Open it, and allow your browser or file manager to install apps if Android asks.

The phone app needs Android 8.0 or newer. The rest of these docs follow Convx for Windows; the phone app has the same features, in the phone’s own menus.

First steps

Convx works straight away: Home, search, moods and the player need no account. To bring your own YouTube Music library, playlists and recommendations, sign in.

  1. Open Settings › Accounts & Services › YouTube Music.
  2. Click Log in. Convx opens a browser window with Google’s sign-in page; sign in there as you normally would.
  3. If your Google account has more than one YouTube channel, pick the one for your library, likes and playlists. You can switch later.

With Auto sync with account, your YouTube Music likes, albums, artists and playlists come into Convx, and what you like or save in Convx goes back. More content adds recommendations from your account to Home. If the browser sign-in doesn’t suit you, Login with token takes the token from the phone app, or a browser’s cookie for music.youtube.com.

Bring your music

From Spotify

Link your account in Settings › Accounts & Services › Spotify to see your Spotify playlists and Liked Songs in the side bar, or use Import from Spotify to copy them into your library. Without Premium, or if Spotify won’t answer, export your playlists as CSV files from exportify.net and import them in Settings › Privacy & Storage › Backup & restore › Import a CSV playlist.

Each song is found on YouTube Music by its title, artist and length. Songs with no close match are skipped, and Convx tells you how many.

From Apple Music, files and playlists

  • Apple Music: import a playlist from its link, or from an export, in Settings › Accounts & Services › Apple Music import.
  • CSV and M3U playlists: Settings › Privacy & Storage › Backup & restore.
  • Your own music files: add folders in Settings › Library & Content › Music folders. SMB and WebDAV network shares work too, and you can leave folders out.

Playing

  • Right-click any song to play it next, add it to the queue, start a radio, make an AI DJ Mix from it, see track insights, pin it to Speed dial, download it, or go to its artist or album.
  • Downloads play with no connection and live in Library under Downloaded. Turn on Auto download on like to keep every liked song, and Save downloads to Music folder for copies in Music\Convx.
  • The queue can carry on with similar songs when it ends, come back after a restart, and remember shuffle and repeat. Find these in Settings › Playback & Audio.
  • Media keys and the play buttons on Convx’s taskbar thumbnail work while Convx is in the background.
  • Recognize listens through your microphone and names the song playing around you, with a history of what it found.

Lyrics

Convx asks every lyrics source at once and shows the first good answer straight away. When a word-timed version arrives, it takes over: letters fill as they’re sung and the line you’re on lifts.

  • Style: 13 lyrics styles, plus position, auto-scroll and swipe-to-skip, in Settings › Player & Lyrics › Lyrics style. Font, size, glow and blur are in Lyrics effects.
  • Sources: choose and order the providers Convx asks in Sources & priority. A PaxSenix or Spicy Lyrics key unlocks more word-synced lyrics.
  • Romanization: show lyrics in Latin letters, under the original or in its place.
  • Translation: add an AI provider and your own API key in AI lyrics translation, then turn it on from the lyrics menu in the player. Each song keeps its translation, so it’s only paid for once per language.
  • Fixing a song: from the lyrics menu, nudge the timing, search for other lyrics, or edit them.

Sound and AutoMix

Equalizer

Settings › Playback & Audio › Equalizer has a Simple three-way dial for bass, mids and treble, and an Advanced view with ten bands and a preamp. Turn the preamp down as far as your biggest boost so loud parts don’t distort. Import Profile loads a ParametricEQ .txt preset made for your headphones, and Spatial audio widens stereo for an out-of-the-head feel.

The same section has audio quality, audio normalization to even out loud and quiet songs, skip silence, and the sound output to use.

AutoMix

In Settings › Playback & Audio › Song transitions, pick AutoMix or a plain crossfade. AutoMix measures each song’s tempo and key, lines up the beats, sweeps filters and plans the handover like a DJ. Creative transitions add reverb, delay, echo freezes and tape stops. Tempo and pitch can be changed for the song playing, from the player.

Player and looks

  • Player design: Card, Vinyl, Clover and the Apple Music designs, with a background of gradient, blur, glow, mesh or glass, and a seek bar that’s default, wavy, slim or a waveform. Settings › Player & Lyrics › Player design.
  • Decorate the player: add stickers, emoji and images around the artwork, then move, size, turn and flip them.
  • Custom player icons: use your own images for the player’s buttons.
  • Liquid Glass: blur, lens and highlights in Settings › Appearance › Liquid Glass. On a slower PC, Reduce live blur helps.
  • App font: Plus Jakarta Sans by default, a handful of others, or install your own .ttf or .otf.
  • Home: choose the tab Convx opens on, show or hide the hero card and mood filters, and reorder Home’s sections.
  • Presets: save a complete look, apply it later, or share it as a file.

Modules

Modules let Convx play songs from other sources, often in lossless FLAC, before falling back to YouTube Music. They work the same way as on the phone.

  1. Open Settings › Library & Content › Modules.
  2. Click Add module source and paste an 8spine module index, or add an Eclipse addon.
  3. Drag the modules into the order you want. The first module that has a song plays it.

Only add modules you trust. Modules are made by other people and fetch songs from wherever their authors chose.

Disc Studio

Disc Studio, in the side bar, designs a CD or a vinyl record for any playlist. The playlist then wears it as its cover.

  1. Click New CD or New vinyl, or start from a preset.
  2. Add layers: a fill, images, text that can curve around the disc, patterns, shapes, stickers from a library of thousands, and your own brush strokes.
  3. Pick the record’s colours, grooves, label size and shine, then click Use on to give it to the playlist.

Designs save as .convxdisc files, so you can share them and open other people’s with Open a design file.

Listen Together

  1. Click the people button at the top right, type the name others will see, and click Create party.
  2. Share the code or the invite link. Friends join with Join party, on a PC or on a phone.
  3. Everyone hears the same song at the same moment, kept in step by the server’s clock.

Turn on Only the host controls playback so guests listen along without pausing, skipping or changing the queue. Songs from files on your own PC can’t be shared, because the others don’t have them.

Discord and scrobbling

Discord

Keep the Discord app open on your PC and turn on Discord in Settings › Accounts & Services › Discord. Your profile shows the song, its artists, the cover and a time bar while it plays, and the status goes away when you pause. The two buttons’ text can use {song_name}, {artist_name} and {album_name}, up to 32 characters.

Last.fm

Last.fm only lets an app scrobble with an API key of its own, so add yours first; Settings › Accounts & Services › Last.fm links to the page that makes one. Then log in and approve Convx on Last.fm’s page. You can also send Now Playing, and love songs on Last.fm when you like them in Convx.

ListenBrainz

Paste your user token from listenbrainz.org/settings. A song is scrobbled once you’ve heard enough of it; paused time doesn’t count.

Backup and storage

  • Backup & restore saves your library, playlists, history and settings to one file, and brings them back. Sign-ins stay on the PC they were made on.
  • Storage shows the room downloads and caches take, sets the largest song and image caches, and clears them. Clearing a cache never touches downloads.
  • Privacy pauses or clears listen and search history, sets when a song counts as a listen, and can keep Convx out of screenshots and screen sharing.

All three are in Settings › Privacy & Storage. Convx keeps its data in %APPDATA%\Convx (library and settings) and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Convx (caches, downloads and logs). More in the Privacy page.

Troubleshooting

  • A song won’t play: Convx asks YouTube for a fresh link and retries on its own. If it still won’t play, try again later, or let a module play it.
  • Convx closed unexpectedly: Settings › Help & About › Crash report shows what happened. Share it on the Discord or in a GitHub issue.
  • Behind a proxy, or having IPv6 trouble: Settings › Accounts & Services › Network & proxy.
  • Convx feels slow: turn on Reduce live blur, or make everything smaller with Display density, in Settings › Player & Lyrics › Controls & layout.
  • Updates: Settings › Help & About › App updates checks for and installs a newer version.

Build it yourself

Convx for Windows is written in Kotlin with Compose Multiplatform. It compiles the phone app’s own YouTube Music, lyrics, artwork, song recognition and module code, decodes audio with FFmpeg, and runs modules in QuickJS. You need JDK 21.

git clone https://github.com/m33md/convx-desktop
cd convx-desktop/desktop
./gradlew run          # start the app
./gradlew packageMsi   # build the installer

The phone app’s code is at cosmictaserdev-creator/Convx. Both are GPL-3.0.
