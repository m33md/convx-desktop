Lyrics

Every word lands right on time.

Convx asks every lyrics source at once and shows the first good answer straight away. When a word-timed version arrives, it takes over: letters fill as they are sung, words lift, and the line you are on sits on a strip of glass.

  • 13 lyric styles, from Flamingo to Karaoke
  • Romanized and translated lyrics, line by line
  • Fix the timing, search or edit the lyrics of any song

Lyrics written for this page. In the app, they come from your song.

One Convx

On your phone, and on your PC.

The same glass, the same red, the same library. Convx on Windows is built from the Android app's own code, so both fetch music, lyrics and artwork the same way.

Android

Where Convx began.

Made by Aryan with Jetpack Compose and Media3, with Cupertino-style controls and liquid glass.

  • Lock-screen, notification and Android Auto controls
  • Songs saved for offline, plus your own files and SMB or WebDAV shares
  • Animated artwork and album colours in the player
Get it on Android
Windows

The official Windows version.

The phone's layout, sized for a big screen: a glass top bar, a simple side bar and a now playing bar that follows you everywhere.

  • Media keys and play controls on the taskbar
  • Several player designs, including a vinyl record
  • Disc Studio, made for a mouse and a big screen
  • Updates itself from inside the app

Songs that blend like a DJ set.

Download

Get Convx. It's free.

Android

Android 8.0 or later
  1. Download the newest APK from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open it, and let your browser install apps if Android asks.
  3. If your phone says “app not compatible”, pick the arm32 APK from the same release.
Download for AndroidAPK · Android 8.0+

Windows

Windows 10 or later, 64-bit
  1. Download Convx-Setup.exe and run it.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. That's it. Convx keeps itself up to date.
