Privacy

Your music, your device.

Convx has no accounts and no servers of its own keeping your data. Here is exactly what it stores, who it talks to, and how to turn things off.

The short version

Convx is a free, open-source music app. There is no Convx account and no Convx server that keeps your data. Your library lives on your device, every sign-in is optional, and you can read every line of the code.

No Convx account

Nothing to sign up for. Convx works the moment you open it.

Your library stays with you

Likes, playlists, history and settings are saved on your device, not on a server of ours.

Every link is optional

YouTube Music, Spotify, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Discord and AI providers only hear from Convx if you turn them on.

No ads or analytics code

Convx for Windows has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Its logs and crash reports stay on your PC.

This page describes Convx for Windows, checked against its source code. The Android app shares its music, lyrics, recognition and party code; see On Android.

What stays on your device

Convx keeps everything it needs on your PC, in two folders:

%APPDATA%\ConvxYour library (likes, playlists, listen and search history), your settings and your downloads.
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ConvxThe song, artwork and lyrics caches, logs and the last crash report.

Stats and Replay are worked out on your PC from that library. Nothing in these folders is sent anywhere unless you send it yourself, for example by sharing a backup file.

Sign-ins and keys

When you sign in to YouTube Music or Spotify, link Last.fm or ListenBrainz, or add an AI provider’s key, Convx saves that sign-in on your PC, encrypted for your Windows user account the way browsers keep their cookies. Each one is only ever sent to the service it belongs to.

Backups made in Settings › Privacy & Storage › Backup & restore hold your library, playlists, history and settings, but never your sign-ins, so a backup file is safe to keep or move.

Who Convx talks to, and when

Convx is an app that plays music from the internet, so it has to talk to other services. Here is each one, when it happens, and what it gets.

YouTube Music
Always
Convx finds and plays music through YouTube Music, so YouTube receives your searches, the songs you play and your IP address, like any YouTube Music app. If you sign in, requests carry your account, and songs you play are added to your YouTube history so Listen again and Home recommendations follow along. Pausing listen history stops that too.
Lyrics providers
When lyrics show
To find lyrics, Convx sends the song’s title, artist, album and length to the lyrics sources turned on in Settings › Player & Lyrics › Sources & priority, such as LRCLIB, KuGou, BetterLyrics, LyricsPlus, SimpMusic and Spicy Lyrics. You choose which ones Convx asks, and in what order.
Song recognition
When you click Recognize
Convx turns a few seconds of what your microphone hears into a sound fingerprint on your PC and sends only that fingerprint to Shazam, which answers with the song. The recording itself isn’t sent.
Listen Together
When you start or join a party
The party server passes the name you choose, the songs, the queue and where playback is to the other people in your party.
Discord
When Discord status is on
Convx hands the song, artist and cover to the Discord app running on your PC, which shows them on your profile. There’s nothing to log into and no Discord password or token is kept.
Last.fm and ListenBrainz
When you link them
The songs you play are scrobbled to your account, and Last.fm can also get your likes. You approve Convx on Last.fm’s own page, so Convx never sees your password.
Spotify and Apple Music
When you link or import
Convx reads the playlists and Liked Songs you choose to bring over, then finds each song on YouTube Music by its title, artist and length.
AI providers
Only with your own API key
AI lyrics translation and the AI features (AI DJ Mix with a vibe, Modify with AI, Track insights) send the lyrics or your request to the provider you picked, such as OpenRouter, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral or DeepL, using the key you entered.
GitHub
When Convx opens
Convx asks GitHub whether a newer version is out. Turn this off in Settings › Help & About › App updates.
Modules
Only if you add one
A module you add fetches songs from the sources its author chose. Modules are made by other people, so only add ones you trust.

Convx isn’t affiliated with any of these services. What they do with what they receive is covered by their own privacy policies.

Your controls

  • Pause listen history stops Convx recording what you play, so it doesn’t show in History, Stats or Home, and isn’t added to your YouTube history. Last.fm and ListenBrainz still scrobble while they’re on.
  • Pause search history stops Convx remembering what you search for.
  • Clear listen history and Clear search history delete what’s saved.
  • Disable screenshot keeps Convx’s window out of screenshots, screen recordings and screen sharing.
  • Storage shows how much room downloads and caches take, and clears them.
  • Sign out of any account, or unlink a service, from Settings › Accounts & Services.

The history controls are in Settings › Privacy & Storage › Privacy. To remove everything, uninstall Convx and delete the two folders above.

On Android

The Android app uses the same YouTube Music, lyrics, artwork, recognition and Listen Together code, so the services above work the same way there. The phone also looks songs up on Apple Music and TIDAL to find animated covers. One more difference: Android has no Discord app to hand your status to, so on a phone Discord status signs in to Discord with your account, and keeps that sign-in on your phone.

Changes and contact

If Convx starts handling data differently, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will say when. Questions are welcome on the Convx Discord, or as an issue on GitHub.
